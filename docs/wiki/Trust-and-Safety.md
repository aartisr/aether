# Trust and Safety

> The current source of truth is [Aether’s privacy and data ethics page](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/privacy).

Trust is a product requirement in Aether, not supporting copy. The project’s public commitments include minimized exposure, accessible design, visible safety boundaries, explainability, and inspectable operational controls.

## Practical boundaries

- Do not treat Aether content as medical diagnosis or emergency advice.
- Keep crisis support explicit and use appropriate local emergency resources when there is immediate danger.
- Do not place personal details, transcripts, audio, contact details, or safety context in analytics events or public issue threads.
- Use [Fairness & Governance](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/fairness-governance) to understand the project’s transparency posture.

For the most current language and public commitments, return to the [canonical Aether website](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/).
