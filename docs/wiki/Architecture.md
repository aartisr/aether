# Aether Architecture

> For the current product experience, visit [Aether](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/).

Aether combines a Next.js product application, local-first reflection tooling, guided resilience experiences, and policy-aware peer-support operations. The architecture is intentionally inspectable: product claims, operational safeguards, and implementation artifacts live together in the [source repository](https://github.com/aartisr/aether).

## Primary public surfaces

- [Resilience Pathway](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/resilience-pathway): check-ins, safety planning, resource navigation, peer circles, and habits.
- [Echo Chamber](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/echo): on-device voice reflection and signal mapping.
- [Fairness & Governance](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/fairness-governance): visibility into matching policy and auditability.
- [Privacy & Data Ethics](https://aether.ai-aarti.com/privacy): privacy boundaries and responsible-design commitments.

For detailed source-of-truth architecture material, see [the architecture guide](https://github.com/aartisr/aether/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md). The main site remains the canonical destination for public-facing product information.
